# Daily opening directions

`opening_director.py` deterministically maps each approved caption format to one self-shot
opening recipe. The direction is written to SQLite before Nano Banana Pro or Kling is called,
so a retry or restart cannot silently change the setting, outfit, or action.

| Hook format | Opening setting | Wardrobe | Silent action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `scorecard` | rumpled bed | black zip hoodie | restrained facepalm |
| `inner-thought` | ordinary kitchen | rust striped tee | brief cover-mouth disbelief |
| `pov` | lived-in bedroom | cream knit | delighted hand-to-chest beat |
| `quote-receipt` | ordinary desk chair | heather lilac tee | confused half-rise |
| `when` | ordinary city walk | olive street layers | caught-off-guard double take |
| `direct-observation` | doorway / bedroom threshold | burgundy long sleeve | compact no-way reaction |
| `reaction-meme` | reclined unmade bed | washed navy tee | dry facepalm disbelief |

All treatments are arm's-length phone-front-camera shots. They start before the payoff, use one
continuous three-second silent action, avoid spoken or mouthed dialogue, reserve clear top space
for the local caption overlay, and reject visible-phone, external-camera, identity-drift, and
anatomy defects during visual review.

The day’s four unique hook formats therefore also result in four different directions, settings,
wardrobes, reactions, and approved flagd demo clips.
