# Hook quality: candid person and phone composition

This prepared round targets the visual quality of the first three seconds. No generation
has been submitted and no production configuration has been changed.

## Fixed elements

- Image model: `gemini-3-pro-image` (Nano Banana Pro).
- Image size: `2K`, vertical `9:16`.
- Identity reference: the approved `master.jpeg`, used for identity rather than its pose,
  expression, hair arrangement or lighting.
- Task: wiping the same small counter area with one free hand.
- Wardrobe: an ordinary grey sweatshirt.
- Setting: a contemporary, middle-class American apartment kitchen.
- Motion: retain the approved task-driven continuation; no new reaction or look-away beat.

## Candidates

1. `close-offset.txt`: closer framing, face off centre and one shoulder cropped.
2. `low-task-view.txt`: a lower phone position, with the cloth and working hand more prominent.
3. `side-task-view.txt`: an oblique phone view, with the face turned toward the task and hair
   falling differently on each side.

Each image prompt stands on its own. Do not append the old generic camera instruction that
requires the camera arm to remain at one edge and never cross the image centre. The near
forearm may be prominent when its perspective is physically plausible; extra or branched
limbs remain unacceptable. Do not inject the hook copy or app-demo description into the image
prompt: the visual task is already specified, and text is added during composition.

## Selection

Compare with the accepted `wipe-counter`, `bedroom-make-bed` and `lean-into-drawer` openings.
Judge the framing and physical relationships before prettiness:

- Does the body position follow the wiping task?
- Is the working hand visibly in contact with the cloth and counter?
- Is the held-phone viewpoint plausible, with one camera arm and one working arm?
- Does her gaze belong to the task, without a presented facial expression?
- Does the frame read as an ordinary video moment rather than a portrait?
- Are lighting, skin, fabric and background believable without artificial degradation?

Still-image selection cannot establish believable motion. Animate only a selected frame,
then inspect the entire short clip for facial acting, frozen posture, object warping and
camera movement. Keep the known motion style fixed during this comparison.
